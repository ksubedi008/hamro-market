Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Hamro Organic, we collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you register for an account or place an order. We also collect transaction information necessary to process your payments securely through eSewa.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for the purpose of fulfilling your orders, providing customer support, and improving your shopping experience. We may use your email to send you important updates regarding your purchases.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. We do not store any sensitive payment credentials directly on our servers; all payments are processed securely via our trusted payment gateway, eSewa.

4. Sharing Your Data

Hamro Organic does not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to others. We may share generic aggregated demographic information not linked to any personal identification information with our trusted affiliates for the purposes outlined above.
